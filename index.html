ABOUT STREETFLOW

StreetFlow turns a street segment into a concept package: an existing cross-section, alternatives that fit between the existing curbs, renderings on a street photo, and an optional planning-level cost estimate.

It runs Fehr & Peers street-concept tools as one guided flow. The drawings use the F&P cross-section asset library; costs use 2025-26 regional bid prices. Outputs are planning-level concepts, not engineering designs.

Built by Adrian Ayala, AICP, Fehr & Peers, Los Angeles.

FAQ

Where does the existing street come from?

OpenStreetMap, checked against the Street View photo. You confirm it and set the curb-to-curb width, which then stays fixed for every concept.

Why do I mark the curbs?

The renderings are placed from the two curb lines on the photo. A person approves them so the concepts land in the right place. AI can suggest them.

What is in the standard package?

The concept renderings sheet and the concept sections sheet, compiled into one PDF. A cost estimate (Excel or PDF) and an AI handoff ZIP are optional.

Can I change a concept after building?

Yes. Go back to Alternatives, edit, and build again. Mark-ups made in Review are saved with the set.

How accurate are the costs?

Planning level only: regional unit prices and typical quantities by delivery tier. They are not an engineer's estimate.
